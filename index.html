💡 What to do if you've been underpaid
  1. Double-check your logged shifts match your rota and clock-in times.
  2. Ask your manager or payroll, politely and in writing if you can, showing your shift list (download the CSV from My shifts).
  3. Underpayments are usually fixed in the next pay run — check it shows up as back pay.
  4. Still stuck? Acas gives free, confidential advice: acas.org.uk · 0300 123 1100.
  5. Being paid below minimum wage can be reported to HMRC: gov.uk/pay-and-work-rights.
